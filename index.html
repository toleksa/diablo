A Diablo-like in the browser — a reimplementation of Diablo III mechanics

Controls:
RMB (hold) — follow the cursor, stop on release  •  LMB — attack with your weapon (an axe or a sword swings; a bow shoots arrows at the cursor, a staff shoots magic missiles for 5 mana and curves only toward what is in front of it)
1 — healing potion (60 s cooldown)  •  2 — your class' opener, named on the skillbar (Mana)  •  3 — your class' finisher (Mana)
Your class' own weapon is already in hand when the run starts — an axe, a sword, a bow or a staff, plain and with nothing on it but its own weight
C — character sheet  •  M — area map (explored parts only)
Click LMB on a skill icon to cast that skill directly
Gather health globes, gold and items. The portal stays dim until the area's overlord is slain — and he is no way to be found in the deepest room: he waits at the gate itself and steps out when you get within half your sight of it — so go where you can see the portal, be ready, then step into it to descend deeper. Ten levels lie under the Crucible; the tenth is the Demon's Roost and its overlord is the last — step into his portal and the run is won, and the window that comes up lets you return to the menu or keep the journey going.
