A Diablo-like in the browser — a reimplementation of Diablo III mechanics

Controls:
RMB (hold) — follow the cursor, stop on release  •  LMB — attack with your weapon (an axe or a sword swings; a bow shoots arrows at the cursor, a staff shoots magic missiles for 5 mana and curves only toward what is in front of it)
1 — healing potion (60 s cooldown)  •  2 — your class' opener, drawn as its own plate and named under it (Mana)  •  3 — your class' finisher, with a plate of its own (Mana)
Your class' own weapon is already in hand when the run starts — an axe, a sword, a bow or a staff, plain and with nothing on it but its own weight
Walking never steers your shots — a bow or a staff still fires along the cursor line while you strafe
C — character sheet  •  M — area map (explored parts only)
Mana - its pool and how fast it fills back - belongs to Intelligence, so a Barbarian holds a little and fills slowly while a Mage holds a lot and fills fast; your own rate is on the character sheet
Click LMB on a skill icon to cast that skill directly
Gather health globes, gold and items. The portal stays dim until the area's overlord is slain — and he is no way to be found in the deepest room: he waits at the gate itself and steps out once the WHOLE gate stands inside the ring of light around your hero — a gate only clipped by the edge of your torchlight leaves him asleep, so go where you can see the portal, be ready, then step into it to descend deeper. Thirteen stages lie under the Crucible: four realms of three levels each, the Roost holding its own three halls, and the thirteenth is the lich king's throne room — there is no gate down at all, so slay the king, let the throne wake, and sit on it to win; the window that comes up lets you return to the menu or keep the journey going.
